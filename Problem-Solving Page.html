OUR APPROACH

Problems Are Opportunities To Improve

Running a successful restaurant means dealing with challenges every day. From busy service periods to customer requests, our team works together to find effective solutions while maintaining our high standards of food and service.

HOW WE HANDLE CHALLENGES

Problems & Solutions

01

Customer Complaints

The Problem: A customer may be unhappy with their meal or service.

Our Solution: We listen carefully, apologise where appropriate and resolve the issue professionally to improve the customer's experience.

02

Busy Restaurant Periods

The Problem: High demand can cause delays and pressure on staff.

Our Solution: We organise staff roles, communicate clearly and work as a team to keep service running smoothly.

03

Food Quality Issues

The Problem: A dish may not meet our expected quality standards.

Our Solution: We check the food, correct the mistake and make sure the kitchen follows our preparation and quality standards.

04

Staff Communication

The Problem: Miscommunication between kitchen and front-of-house staff can cause mistakes.

Our Solution: We encourage clear communication, regular updates and teamwork between all departments.

OUR METHOD

How We Solve Problems

01

Identify the Problem

Understand what has happened and why it needs attention.

02

Consider the Options

Discuss possible solutions and choose the most suitable one.

03

Take Action

Work together to put the solution into practice quickly and professionally.

04

Review the Result

Check that the problem has been resolved and learn from the experience.
